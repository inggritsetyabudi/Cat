C@ Vision and Mission

1. Design Decisions

Vision
C@ becomes the default choice for teams that need systems-level control without accepting unsafe defaults or high language complexity.

Mission
C@ delivers:
- Native-speed execution
- Deterministic memory management
- Readable source code
- Auditable safety boundaries
- A production-grade compiler and toolchain

Operational mission
- Replace unsafe low-level code where memory safety is required.
- Replace GC-based infrastructure components where latency and memory control matter.
- Provide a simpler systems language for backend and platform teams.

Success profile
A C@ project should be understandable by a new engineer after reading a small language core, not after mastering advanced ownership notation.

2. Rationale

FAST
- The mission targets services, runtime infrastructure, protocol stacks, storage engines, build tools, and OS-adjacent components where predictable performance matters.

SAFE
- Production systems cannot depend on “expert-only safe usage” of unsafe primitives.

SIMPLE
- The language must remain teachable to teams, not only to specialists.

3. Constraints

- The language may not depend on GC to achieve memory safety.
- The language may not outsource safety to discipline or style guides.
- The language may not require users to manually express lifetime graphs.
- The mission does not include being the most feature-rich language.

4. Trade-offs

- Some advanced abstractions will arrive later than in larger ecosystems.
- The language prioritizes reliability and clarity over novelty for its own sake.
- V1 favors a narrow, stable core over maximal flexibility.
