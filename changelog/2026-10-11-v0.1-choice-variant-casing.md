# 2026-10-11 — v0.1 choice-variant casing

Compiler now resolves choice variant names exactly instead of case-insensitively, rejects custom variants beginning with uppercase letters (with a lowercase suggestion), and reports a mismatched constructor as an unknown choice variant when its expected type is known. The closed capitalized names `Ok`, `Fail`, `Some`, and `None` remain permitted.

Updated the positive choice-resolution fixture to use lowercase custom variants and canonical `Ok`/`Fail`; added negative declaration and constructor-case fixtures, and refreshed AIR/OIR goldens from the compiler output. This change does not mark the full pattern grammar or PRD section complete.

Validation: a clean temporary CMake build succeeded; the PRD doc-test slice passed (2 tagged blocks: 1 success and 1 expected rejection), and the full frontend, OIR, backend, and native suites passed. No commit or push was made. Arena and module-management/exposure behavior were not changed.
